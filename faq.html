Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about commissions, pricing, production, shipping, and the overall process.

How long does a commission take?

Production time depends on the complexity of the project. You'll receive regular progress updates throughout the process.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! Fluffistry ships worldwide using secure packaging and trusted shipping services.

Do you accept payment plans?

Payment plans may be available depending on the project size. Feel free to ask before ordering.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Minor revisions during the design stage are included to make sure you're happy with the result.

How do I start a commission?

Simply contact us through the Contact page with your character references and project details.
