QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Everything You Might Want To Know

Transparency is part of every commission. Here are the questions we receive most often before a project begins.

How long does a commission usually take?

Every project is unique, but most commissions are completed within 4–8 weeks depending on complexity, materials and our current queue.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We accept international commissions and ship worldwide.

Can I commission only a reference sheet?

Absolutely! You can order a reference sheet without purchasing a fursuit.
